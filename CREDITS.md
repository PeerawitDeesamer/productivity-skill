## Updating one

Updates are by hand. Copy the folder from upstream, update the commit in the table and in the
folder's `NOTICE.md`, and check the upstream license is still MIT for that path.
(caveman's repo is MIT except its engine directories, which are BSL-1.1. `skills/caveman` is MIT.)
